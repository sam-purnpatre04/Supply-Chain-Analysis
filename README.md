# 📦 Supply Chain Analysis & Performance Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations to identify patterns and insights related to sales, products, customers, suppliers, warehouses, shipping performance, delivery delays, and returns.

The project uses data analysis and visualization techniques to understand operational performance and identify areas where supply chain efficiency can be improved.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to present the analysis and provide meaningful business insights.

---

##  Business Problem

Supply chain teams need to monitor sales, delivery performance, supplier efficiency, product demand, and customer satisfaction to ensure smooth operations.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

- Which products and categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions contribute the most sales?
- Which suppliers have the best performance?
- Which shipping modes experience the most delays?
- How frequently are orders delayed?
- Which products or categories have higher return rates?
- How does delivery performance affect customer ratings?
- Which areas require operational improvement?

##  Key Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-performing product categories and products.
- Regions contributing significantly to overall revenue.
- Suppliers with strong or weak operational performance.
- Shipping modes associated with higher delivery delays.
- Products and categories with higher return rates.
- Areas where delivery performance can be improved.
- Relationship between operational performance and customer satisfaction.

---

##  Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

- Improve supplier performance monitoring.
- Optimize shipping modes based on delivery performance.
- Identify causes of recurring delivery delays.
- Focus inventory planning on high-demand products.
- Monitor products with high return rates.
- Improve warehouse and logistics planning.
- Use customer ratings to identify service-quality issues.
