# 📦 Supply Chain & Delivery Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed 3,000+ orders generating ₹74.17M in revenue to evaluate delivery performance, revenue trends, supplier performance, returns, and customer satisfaction.

## 🎯 Objective

To identify supply chain bottlenecks, understand delivery issues, and generate actionable insights to improve operational and customer performance.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- DAX

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard provides an overview of revenue, delivery performance, supplier delays, shipping modes, regions, returns, and customer ratings.

![Supply Chain Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## 🔍 Key KPIs

- **Total Orders:** 3,000+
- **Total Revenue:** ₹74.17M
- **On-Time Delivery:** 62.60%
- **Average Delay:** 0.88 days
- **Average Customer Rating:** 3.94 / 5

## 💡 Key Insights

- **62.60%** of orders were delivered on time, while a significant portion experienced delays.
- Electronics, Grocery, and Clothing were among the major revenue-generating categories.
- Supplier performance varied based on average delivery delays.
- Delivery performance and return patterns were analyzed to identify potential operational issues.
- Overall customer satisfaction was **3.94/5**.

## 🚧 Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge:** Raw data contained multiple dimensions such as products, suppliers, regions, shipping modes, delivery, and returns, making it difficult to identify clear business patterns.

**Solution:** Cleaned and analyzed the data using Python and created business-focused KPIs and visualizations in Power BI.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Monitor suppliers with higher delivery delays.
- Investigate major causes of delayed orders.
- Optimize shipping modes based on delivery performance.
- Monitor returns and customer ratings alongside delivery performance.
- Focus on maintaining reliable supply for high-revenue categories.
